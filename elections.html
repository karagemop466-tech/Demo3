Elections

Tracking live Kalshi election events (highest-dollar) until each event resolves to yes or no — e.g. next President / Prime Minister / party winners. Each row links to its official settlement sources and the exact demo-API endpoint it was captured from.

Resolution tracking

Each event is tracked live until resolved. When the event resolves, the outcome is recorded as Yes or No per Kalshi's settlement rules and the linked settlement source. No outcomes are pre-filled here.
