01 · Data definition

Keep volume, open interest, and displayed liquidity separate

DECISION NEEDED

Show contract counts beside the derived top quote value, and ask the owner to select the primary ranking metric before describing an event as “highest dollar amount.”

Benefit
Avoids calling a contract count or a top quote a dollar turnover total.
Evidence
Official market and order-book values; metric definition approved by owner.

02 · Security boundary

Use a narrow server-side account gateway

REQUIRED BEFORE ACCOUNT SYNC

Keep signing material outside browser code and Pages artifacts. Start with read-only balance/positions/fills; add order entry only after a separate scope and risk-control decision.

Benefit
Protects the private key and makes account access auditable.
Evidence
Approved hosting, secret storage, account/subaccount scope, and signed-request tests.

03 · Event lifecycle

Add durable watchlist monitoring after a backend exists

NOT BACKGROUND YET

Persist the chosen market/event identifiers, capture timestamps, and re-check official status/result until settlement. The current browser watchlist cannot run while the tab is closed.

Benefit
Supports a durable audit trail from open status through final result.
Evidence
Retention policy, event-state handling, reconnect rules, and historical-cutoff coverage.

04 · Resolution quality

Show market rules and settlement sources with the outcome

TRANSPARENCY

Expose the event's settlement-source links, rules, result, status, and settlement timestamp side by side. Preserve scalar outcomes instead of forcing them into yes/no.

Benefit
Lets a reviewer inspect the contract terms behind a displayed resolution.
Evidence
Returned event/market rules and settlement-source records.

05 · Trade analysis

Separate realized P&L, unrealized P&L, fees, and cash movement

ACCOUNTING DEFINITION

Do not summarize performance from price movement alone. Reconcile fills, current positions, balance, and fees; document which accounts and time boundaries are included.

Benefit
Makes strategy comparisons auditable and avoids blending different measures.
Evidence
Authenticated fills, positions, balance, timestamps, and an agreed formula.
Balance ↗ · Positions ↗ · Fills ↗
Review status

06 · Data quality

Mark stale, missing, and incomplete data rather than carrying it forward

QUALITY GATE

Display observation time, raw source link, missing-field state, and API connection status. Reject incomplete top-quote aggregates from dollar ranking instead of estimating a replacement.

Benefit
Prevents stale values and partial books from appearing current or complete.
Evidence
Source timestamps, API response fields, and documented freshness thresholds.
