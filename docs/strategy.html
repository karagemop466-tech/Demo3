Prerequisites before anyone trades on this

  1. D13 — agree the P&L definition (realised vs. settlement revenue vs. fees).
  2. D9 — widen the settlement sample beyond the current window via the historical endpoints.
  3. D12 — name who is accountable for automated order entry, and the size limit.
  4. Compute fees. A 1.58¢ edge is smaller than many fee schedules; series carry fee_type and fee_multiplier.
  5. Do not assume demo results transfer — Kalshi states demo prices may not reflect real markets (Demo Environment).
