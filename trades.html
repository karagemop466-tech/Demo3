Trades — execution & log

Execute trades on events against the Kalshi demo exchange (live mode with your demo API key) or in paper mode (default: simulated fills at the live best bid/ask, clearly labeled — nothing is sent to Kalshi). Every trade is journaled, categorized and analyzed on Portfolio.

Execution guide

StepWhat happensSource
1. Pick an event on the Live dashboard Ranking shows the highest estimated dollar volume; Track loads chart + orderbook. source
2. Choose side & limit price YES and NO prices are complementary (NO ≈ 1 − YES). Use the orderbook to size at each price. source
3. Place the order Live: POST /trade-api/v2/portfolio/events/orders (RSA-PSS/Ed25519 signed). Paper: filled locally at live quote. source
4. Track to resolution Markets settle yes/no; winners receive $1/contract. Results feed the win-rate analysis. source
