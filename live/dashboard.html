Liquidity — contracts available at each price

Orderbook levels from Get Market Orderbook: the response publishes YES bids and NO bids; a NO bid at price P is a YES ask at (1 − P), which is how the depth chart is built. Green bars are YES bids, red bars are NO bids shown at their YES-equivalent price.

Tracked lines by topic

Topics are kept separate and each topic's events are grouped together. Rows are the top-dollar lines per topic plus every contract that has committed candlestick history. Click a ticker to load it in the graph.

Resolution tracking (YES / NO)

Open events stay listed until they settle; resolved events show how they resolved. Full settlement data: the collector's tracking page.
