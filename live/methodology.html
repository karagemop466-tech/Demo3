Methodology & verification

Every number on this site is one of four things, and each is labelled: published (copied from the API), derived (a published formula over published fields), modelled (this project's assumption) or simulated (paper trading). Nothing else is shown.

1 · Where the data comes from

ExchangeKalshi demo — https://external-api.demo.kalshi.co/trade-api/v2 (environments · demo env)
AuthNone for market data (docs); no credentials exist in this repo, so nothing authenticated is shown (Get Balance).
MarketsGet Markets — snapshot from the scheduled collector, committed in data/latest/.
CandlesticksGet Market Candlesticks — period_interval 1, 60 or 1440 minutes. Fields used: price.open/high/low/close_dollars, volume_fp.
OrderbookGet Market Orderbook — orderbook_fp.yes_dollars / no_dollars as [price_dollars, contract_count].
SettlementMarket settlement — $1 per winning contract; result ∈ {yes, no}.
Fixed-point fieldsFixed-point migration — *_fp fields are decimal strings; they are parsed, never rounded to cents.
Rate limitsRate limits — the dashboard spaces requests and backs off on 429.

3 · Alerts and the "what caused it" question

An alert fires when a committed candlestick shows an absolute move of at least $0.10 either close-to-close over the window, or high-to-low inside a single candle. Each alert links:

  • the exact candlestick request that produced the numbers (official),
  • the market record for the contract (official),
  • a Google News search for the contract's subject around that date.

The headline link is a search, not an established cause. Proving that a headline caused a move would need article-level timestamps and an event study with a baseline; the desk does not claim that, and says so in flag L-08.

4 · Simulated trading

No order is ever sent. Fills are modelled at quoted prices, exits are ±$0.10 or resolution, and results are computed with the documented settlement arithmetic. The three strategies and their exact rules are on Strategies. Fees are not modelled (flag L-06). The simulation is deterministic: rebuilding from the same committed data produces the same trades.

5 · What this build does not do

  • It cannot open an authenticated WebSocket from a static page (WS auth).
  • It does not report a demo account balance, positions or fills — no credentials (flag L-07).
  • It does not claim a dollar-volume field exists; the ranking is derived (flag L-03).
  • It does not fill gaps in the candles or interpolate missing prices.
  • It does not assert that news caused a move (flag L-08).

6 · Reproduce it

python3 scripts/build_live_candles.py   # candlestick store from committed official payloads
python3 scripts/build_live_data.py      # alerts, model, simulation, registers
python3 scripts/build_live_site.py      # these pages
python3 scripts/verify_live_site.py     # links, per-row sources, JSON shape, JS syntax

The dev sandbox for this session had no HTTPS egress to the Kalshi API (TLS handshake refused), so candlestick payloads were reused from the repository's committed collector artifacts rather than re-fetched. GitHub Actions runners do have egress; the scheduled collector keeps refreshing data/.
