The three changes that matter most

  1. Server-side alerting — alerts must exist before the page opens, otherwise the desk is only live while someone is watching it.
  2. Deeper one-minute history — the $0.10 rule needs dense candles; today's coverage cannot produce alerts in gas, crypto or golf even though those markets move.
  3. Fee-aware ROI — a model that ignores fees will flatter itself; once fees are verified the high-ROI filter becomes actionable rather than indicative.
