Regularities and irregularities of the underlying data

Two things about the demo exchange are worth knowing before reading any table:

  • Synthetic self-test series. KXDEMOFUTHOURLY ("Demo BTC at or above …") and KXQUICKSETTLE are exchange tests. They rank high by volume and are excluded here (series record).
  • Placeholder candles. When a market does not trade in a period the API can emit a candle with previous_dollars and no close_dollars. Those are stored as gaps, not prices (docs).

Kalshi Live Alert Desk — asks only about the Kalshi demo exchange (https://external-api.demo.kalshi.co/trade-api/v2). Every table row links its official source request; values that cannot be confirmed are left blank and listed on Flagged for review. Simulated trades are labelled SIMULATED everywhere and are never sent to Kalshi.
