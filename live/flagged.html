L-04ModelThe ROI score is a heuristic, not a measured returnp_hat is built from the quoted midpoint, one hour of price momentum and the spread. The formula is published on the page; it is a way to prioritise what to look at and makes no claim about realised returns.needs review1 · 2
L-05SimulationSimulated fills are modelled at quoted prices and fees are not includedEvery simulated trade fills as a taker at the candlestick's quoted side price, exits at +$0.10, -$0.10 or resolution, and pays $1 per winning contract per the settlement docs. Exchange fees are not modelled because the demo fee schedule was not verified for this environment.needs review1 · 2
L-06FeesFee model absent (unverified for the demo environment)No fee schedule for the demo exchange was confirmed from an official Kalshi page during this build, so fees are recorded as 0.00 and labelled, never estimated.needs review1
L-07CredentialsNo demo API credentials are configured in this repositoryPortfolio reads and live demo order entry need a demo API key (KALSHI_DEMO_API_KEY_ID / KALSHI_DEMO_PRIVATE_KEY). L-08CausationNews attribution is a link, not a proven causeEach alert shows an official source link for the price data and a search link for headlines around that timestamp. A headline is never asserted to have caused the move; the page says the association is unverified.needs review1
L-09CoverageCandlestick history is limited to the tracked lines46 contracts have committed candlestick history; others are tracked by quote only. Coverage is stated on the page rather than implied.needs review1
L-12CoverageSome topics had no committed $0.10 swing during the collected windowsAlerts are only ever produced from committed official candlesticks. Gas, crypto and golf contracts in the archive traded in increments smaller than $0.10 over the collected windows, so they appear on the topic pages with their quotes and candles but with no swing alerts. Nothing is invented to fill the gap; the scheduled collector widens the history over time.needs review1
L-11Environment mixingSome candlestick history comes from the production API archive, labelleddata/latest/candles.json and data/history/ hold demo-exchange payloads. Those contracts are copied read-only and carry environment='production' in every table and chart caption so demo and production data are never confused.needs review1

Verified correct (spot-checked line by line)

  • Demo + WS roots match demo environments.
  • Orderbook is bids-only, ascending, best = last, YES ask = 1 − best NO bid (orderbook responses); implemented identically here.
  • Candlestick path and period_interval ∈ {1, 60, 1440} (candlesticks).
  • $1 per winning contract and zero settlement fee for simple yes/no (settlement).
  • Signed message = timestamp + METHOD + path-without-query; Ed25519 direct / RSA-PSS-SHA256 (API keys).
  • 429 backoff for unauthenticated requests (rate limits).
