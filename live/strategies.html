Strategies & trade categories

Three strategies trade the collected candlesticks automatically — there is no manual input. Everything here is SIMULATED: fills are modelled at the quoted price, exits at ±$0.10 or resolution, and no order is ever sent to Kalshi (no demo credentials exist in this repository — flag L-07).

Strategy definitions

StrategyRule (entry and exit)Source
S1 Swing momentumTrade in the direction of a 1-hour move of at least $0.10; enter at the quoted ask/bid, exit at +$0.10, -$0.10, or resolution.settlement rules
S2 Fade the swingTrade against a 1-hour move of at least $0.10 (mean reversion); same exits as S1.settlement rules
S3 Model edgeTake the side with the largest modelled edge (p_hat vs quoted ask/bid) when the edge is at least $0.03 per contract; same exits.settlement rules

Rules are this project's, not Kalshi's; only the fill/settlement semantics come from the docs.

How a simulated fill is priced 
entry  : taker at the candle's quoted side price (buy YES at the ask, buy NO at 1 − bid)
exit   : +$0.10 target · −$0.10 stop · or resolution ($1 for the winning side, $0 otherwise)
size   : contracts = $10 risk / $0.10 stop = 100 contracts per position
re-entry: same line and strategy at most once per 6 hours, never while a position is open
fees   : not modelled (no verified demo fee schedule) — flag L-06
source : https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/market_settlement · https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/market/get-market-candlesticks
