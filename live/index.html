Kalshi Live Alert Desk

A live desk for the Kalshi demo exchange covering five topics only — gas prices, elections, culture, crypto, golf. It ranks the highest-dollar live events, alerts on any price swing of at least $0.10 in either direction in two scrolling streams, tracks events until they resolve YES or NO, and runs auto-simulated trades to test strategies against the same official data.

What to look at first

1 · The two alert streams

Both stream every swing ≥ $0.10 with the contract, direction, both prices, the window, volume and links to the exact official candlestick request plus a headline search for that timestamp.

  • All swing alerts — every alert in the five topics.
  • High-ROI alerts — the same events filtered by the documented EV model (EV ≥ $0.03 per contract and ROI ≥ 10%).

2 · Highest-dollar events by topic

Each topic has its own page with its events grouped together (see the topic nav). Dollar volume is derived (contracts × notional) because the trade API publishes no dollar-volume field — flagged as L-03.

3 · Price graph & liquidity

The graph page plots contract price against time with windows from 30 seconds to 30 days, and shows how many contracts are available at each price from the orderbook.

4 · Resolution tracking

Resolution tracking keeps every event until it settles and records how it resolved — YES or NO — with the settlement source.

5 · Simulated strategies

Three strategies trade automatically on the collected candlesticks (strategies, portfolio). No credentials exist in this repo, so nothing is ever sent to Kalshi; every fill says SIMULATED.

Live polling: the dashboard calls the public demo API from your browser and says so when it succeeds or is blocked. The committed snapshot is always shown with its timestamp, never presented as live data. Reason: the browser cannot open an authenticated Kalshi WebSocket (flag L-10), and some networks block the API (flag L-01).

How this keeps itself honest

  • Every table row has a source link, or says unverified.
  • Derived numbers are labelled derived; modelled numbers (ROI model, simulated fills) say model or SIMULATED and publish their formula on Methodology.
  • Irregularities are published with evidence links on Flagged for review — currently 13 items.
  • The scripts that rebuild every number are in this repo: scripts/build_live_candles.py, scripts/build_live_data.py, scripts/verify_live_site.py.
