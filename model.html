High-ROI screen — definition and measured demo context

The second scrolling chat on the live dashboard is meant to show "events that our model likes as high ROI". No model was defined in this repo, so this page publishes the screen actually used, plus every number measured from the committed demo snapshots that backs it. It is a ranking aid for what to look at first, not a return forecast.

1 · Screen definition

Deterministic screen for the second scrolling chat. A swing alert is admitted only when the price move is at least $0.10 in either direction (the brief's threshold) AND the move is at least 10% of the prior price, so a 1c->11c jump on a longshot is not treated the same as a 45c->55c jump on a coin-flip. No edge is claimed.

3 · Calibration feasibility (read this before trusting any ROI number)

insufficient: a >= $0.10 move has to be measured BEFORE the settlement print. The settled snapshot carries only `previous_price_dollars` and `last_price_dollars`, and 340 of its 341 qualifying moves have a prior price below $0.05 - the field is not a tick-by-tick price path. Recorded candles cover 12 markets, so no threshold can be fitted or back-tested on this data. Publishing a calibrated ROI number here would be a fabrication.

Candle windows are recorded for 12 market(s) in data/latest/candles.json (field semantics: Get Market Candlesticks); the minimum used here for any fit claim is 30 series with a pre-settlement price path.

5 · Caveats and limits

  • Demo environment only (mock funds). Kalshi warns demo prices and behaviour may not reflect real markets: https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/demo_env
  • Entry-price proxy is `last_price_dollars` on a settled market, i.e. the last trade before resolution; it is not an achievable fill for a live alert. Figures are descriptive of the snapshot, not a forecast.
  • ROI of a winning YES contract uses the $1.00 payout per https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/market_settlement; fees are not modelled because the docs do not state a demo fee schedule (board flag ST-U1).
  • Every rate above is a raw count on the snapshot; n < 25 in a band means that band is not reported as a rate at all (reported=false).
  • The screen ranks events to look at. It is not investment advice and not a verified return model.

Open items are also published on Flagged for review; the requirement each one belongs to is on Requirements.
