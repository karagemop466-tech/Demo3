Live Dashboard

Tracks the highest-dollar live Kalshi events until they resolve (yes / no). Contract price is graphed against time; you can choose the X-axis length (seconds / minutes / hours). Y axis = current contract price. Current liquidity (contracts available at a given price) is shown alongside.

X axis = time (selectable seconds/minutes/hours above). Y axis = current contract price ($0–$1). The right axis shows liquidity: how many contracts are available at the current ask price. Live feed reads from the official demo API; if cross-origin fetching is blocked, verified snapshot data is plotted and the gap is noted below.
