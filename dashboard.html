6 · Price-swing alerts — scrolling chat (≥ $0.10)

Live alerts for contract price swings of ≥ $0.10 in either direction, detected from the dashboard's live market samples (GET /markets, demo API, no key). Two scrolling chats: All events shows every swing; High ROI model shows the selective screen published on High-ROI model (definition + the rates measured from the committed demo snapshots, thresholds labelled uncalibrated — never presented as verified ROI). The full requirement-by-requirement map of this site is on Requirements. Each row links its exact market API URL and, in Context, the event's official settlement_sources (Get Event). Those are resolution sources, not proof of cause: each row says "Cause not verified". Each alerted market is then followed (batched GET /markets?tickers=…&limit=100, every 20 s) until the exchange reports result; the chat then shows RESOLVED YES (→ $1.00) or RESOLVED NO (→ $0.00) with the official settlement_value_dollars and settlement_ts. While result is empty the row stays open — no outcome is guessed. Alerts persist in this browser only.
