2 · Live price-swing alerts

The all-events feed records each scanned market whose observed price changes by at least $0.10 up or down between two successful scans. It uses the YES quote midpoint when a valid bid/ask pair exists; otherwise it uses the last traded price. The first scan seeds a baseline. This is REST polling, not a continuous tick feed; the elapsed time between observations is shown. Coverage follows the scan-depth selection above and is limited to the five tracked topics. Each alert links to the official Kalshi market and event records. Where returned, Kalshi-listed settlement-source websites and market resolution rules are shown as context; they are not evidence that a site caused the price move (see I-19). Feed history is stored in this browser only.

3 · Automated paper-strategy lab

Simulation only; no Kalshi order is sent. On a qualifying alert, this browser automatically records one simulated YES contract for the matching baseline: Momentum YES after an upward move, or Pullback YES after a downward move. The assumed entry is the displayed YES ask; the top-level size/fill is not verified. Open positions are marked at the displayed YES bid (last trade only if no bid is available) and checked for resolution through the official market endpoint. Gross P&L excludes fees and slippage. Local results persist in this browser only. This is an exploratory rules test, not a profitability claim; demo prices may not reflect real markets (see I-20).

6 · Demo account (optional) & trading

A Kalshi demo API key (from demo.kalshi.co → Account Settings → API Keys; see API keys) enables live demo trading and portfolio reads. Without a key the ticket runs in paper mode for the manual ticket (an instant simulated fill at the entered limit price; nothing is sent to Kalshi). The separate auto strategy lab assumes an entry at the displayed YES ask and labels results as simulated. Your key never leaves this browser (localStorage).
