Rules & methodology

No ledger yet. data/paper/ is created by the first run of .github/workflows/paper-desk.yml (scheduled every 20 minutes on the default branch, or on demand). The pages below stay valid and fill in as cycles complete; nothing on this site is back-filled by hand.

What one desk cycle does

  1. Load the tracked universe for the five focus topics from the collector snapshot data/latest/top_events.json (top events per topic, top markets per event; synthetic demo series excluded).
  2. Refresh quotes with GET /markets?tickers=… in batches of 50 — the documented behaviour is that when tickers is the only filter, every matching market comes back in one response, no paging.
  3. Apply exit rules to open positions (take-profit +0.20 / stop-loss −0.20 / time-stop), then check whether any resting order is crossed by the published ask.
  4. Evaluate the five entry rules; sort candidates by the size of the move (largest first), then by ticker, so the run is reproducible.
  5. Simulate fills: taker at the published best ask (buy) or best bid (sell), capped by the contracts resting at that price and by the risk caps; anything else is recorded as a rejection with a reason. Maker orders rest and only fill on a later crossing quote.
  6. Realize P&L where the market has resolved (result yes/no, $1 per winning contract), mark everything else at the current bid, append one equity sample, and commit the ledger plus this regenerated subsite.

F-EB2-03No fee model.Trading fees are not applied to simulated fills, so realized P&L is gross. Kalshi’s settlement doc confirms a zero settlement fee for simple yes/no markets (source); per-trade maker/taker fees are deliberately not modeled here rather than guessed.ledger sources
F-EB2-04The API has no sub-minute history.Candlesticks are published at 1 / 60 / 1440 minutes only (Get Market Candlesticks), so a seconds-granularity x-axis can only come from the browser’s own live polling, not from history; the chart selector says so.ledger sources
F-EB2-05Quotes can lag by seconds.The markets endpoint documents short response caching, so a cycle-to-cycle delta is a lower bound on the true move (doc text). WebSocket streaming would remove the lag but needs signed handshake headers a browser cannot attach (board flag I-13).ledger sources
F-EB2-06Cause of a price move is never asserted.Kalshi’s public API publishes no news feed. This ledger records the market’s own rules_primary text and timestamps; attributing a jump to a specific website link would be inference, so it is out of scope here and left to the alerting work owned by other sessions on the board.ledger sources
F-EB2-07Synthetic demo series excluded.The demo catalog contains exchange test series (e.g. hourly BTC demos, “1+1=2” markets, “shard” placeholders). They are excluded from the tradeable universe by name in trading/paper_engine.py; the excluded list and its evidence live in data/paper/sources.json.ledger sources
