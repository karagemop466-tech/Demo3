Paper desk summary

Automated simulated trading on Kalshi’s demo exchange, five focus topics: gas prices, elections, culture, crypto, golf.

The desk runs unattended: it reads the tracked universe from the collector snapshot, pulls live quotes from the official demo REST root https://external-api.demo.kalshi.co/trade-api/v2, applies five fixed rules in trading/paper_strategies.py, and simulates fills against the published top-of-book prices with only the contracts actually resting at that price. No order is ever sent; no credentials are used (public market data needs none per the docs).
