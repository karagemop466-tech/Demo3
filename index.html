What the dashboard does

CapabilityHow it worksSource
Topic discovery Fetches all series via GET /series, classifies into the 5 topics by keyword match on title/tags/categories. No hard-coded tickers. Get Series List
Highest-dollar ranking Open markets per series (GET /markets?series_ticker=&status=open), ranked by estimated $ volume = contracts × last price. Get Markets
Price chart X = time, Y = contract price. Windows: 60s → 24h. Live poll samples + 1-min candlesticks (period_interval=1). Get Market Candlesticks
Liquidity / depth Order book bids per price level (contracts available at each price), spread, resting liquidity. Bids only — YES bid X ≡ NO ask (1−X). Orderbook Responses
Resolution tracking Logs markets as they close/settle with result = yes/no. Market Settlement
Portfolio & trading Optional demo key: balance, positions, fills, orders; place/cancel V2 orders; fills categorized by topic × side × price band × maker/taker for strategy review. Get Balance

How to use

  1. Open the dashboard — market data loads with no key.
  2. Review discovery (Section 1) and the ranked table (Section 2).
  3. Click Track on a market for its chart, order book, and trades (Section 3).
  4. To trade: create a demo account at demo.kalshi.co, generate an API key, then connect in Section 5. Demo funds only.
  5. Review Suggestions and Open Questions before changing strategy.
