How the data gets here

A GitHub Actions job calls the Kalshi demo REST API, selects at most 14 series per topic by series volume, pulls open and settled markets, order books and candlesticks, and commits a compact artifact. analysis/build.py then computes per-topic aggregates, the settlement study and the strategy rules, and tools/check_site.py plus tools/render_test.js refuse to ship a page with a broken link, an uncited row or a table that renders empty.

Kalshi states that demo prices may not reflect real markets; when credentials are absent the portfolio section says so rather than showing zero. Both points are on the Flagged for review page.
