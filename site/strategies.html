Strategies derived from trade types

Two independent views of the same ledger. Inferred style groups each fill by observable features — the API's own fields, bucketed by fixed rules. Declared strategy groups by the label the operator stamped on the order. Where they disagree, or where a bucket has too few settled trades to mean anything, the verdict is insufficient data — never a guess dressed as a result.

Inferred style — scoreboard

A row is “confirmable” only at ≥10 settled trades (STRATEGY_TESTS in scripts/analyze.py). trades_observed counts every matching row; settled counts only those whose market has a result.

Read the sample size before believing a row. With 5 paper fills and 0 settled outcomes, every hit-rate on this page is structurally undefined. That is the honest state of the project, not a bug to average away: a strategy verdict needs settled trades on the same market type, and Kalshi demo markets resolve on their own schedule.
