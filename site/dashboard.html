Latency and limits. Kalshi documents that REST responses are cached for a short time — “prices, sizes and status can lag the exchange by several seconds; filtered listings can lag longer” (source). Unauthenticated traffic is rate-limited separately (source), so keep the refresh interval at 15 s or slower. True sub-second streaming needs the signed WebSocket bridge (source).
