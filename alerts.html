Website links that may explain a sharp jump

From the official Kalshi API only: every event carries settlement_sources (the sites that determine the outcome) and every series carries tags/categories. When an alert fires we link those official sites for that event, so a jump can be checked against the site that actually settles it. These are candidate causes, not verified explanations — nothing is asserted as the cause of a move.
