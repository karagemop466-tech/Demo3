Live swing alerts

Two scrolling chats, newest at the bottom. All swings lists every tracked market that moves by $0.10 or more (up or down) between observations. High-ROI screen lists only swings that pass a rule-based screen, plus resolutions. The chats are fed by the official Kalshi demo API from your browser (no key); if the browser is blocked, the collector's snapshot backfill still shows.
