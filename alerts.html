Live alerts — ≥ $0.10 swings, two scrolling chats

The alert engine polls the five tracked topics live in your browser from the Kalshi demo API (public market data needs no key — Quick start: market data) and posts a message the moment a watched contract price moves by the chosen threshold (default $0.10) in either direction between polls. Chat 1 shows every swing on every watched market; chat 2 filters to markets the published heuristic ROI model likes (high potential upside × resting liquidity — an unverified heuristic, flag F-99E28-01, not investment advice). Markets are tracked until they resolve; the yes/no result is posted to both chats. Each alert links its exact official API URL, the event's official settlement sources, and a clearly labeled news-search link used only to investigate what may have caused the jump (an investigation link, never a claim of cause).

High-ROI model — published gates (heuristic, unverified)

Deterministic filter evaluated from fields of the official Get Markets response. Every gate must pass for chat 2 to show the swing. The model is a heuristic estimate, not a verified Kalshi metric (flag F-99E28-01); tune the gates in assets/alerts.js (KalshiAlerts.MODEL).

Resolution tracking (yes / no) — until every event resolves

When a watched market settles, the result is posted to both chats and logged here (this browser's log; the dashboard's log is shared via the same storage). The sweep re-polls every watched series with status=settled every 5th scan and reads the most recent settled markets per series (limit 200) — a settled market outside that window is reported by the next sweep that sees it, never inferred. Seeded rows come from the collector snapshot with their captured API URLs.
