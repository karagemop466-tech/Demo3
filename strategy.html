Strategy lab — measured results

Simulated trades run over recorded Kalshi demo data already committed to this repository, with published rules, a documented fill model and the official fee formula. Every number below is reproducible offline by python3 scripts/backtest_strategies.py.

Read this first. Two datasets are reported and never mixed: Resolved (4 trades on 2 gas markets that actually settled YES/NO) and Exploratory (23,531 trades on 81 recorded series, marked to market because no settlement exists for them in this repository). The resolved sample is honest but tiny; the exploratory sample is large but cannot be settled. Neither is a live edge, and the demo environment is not the real exchange: Kalshi documents that the demo environment uses mock funds and that demo prices may not reflect real markets.

How the events were resolved (YES / NO)

These are the only series in the repository with both a recorded price path and a documented settlement. The settlement values are the official settlement_value_dollars field (“Yes outcome: Yes contract holders receive $1 per contract”, Market Settlement). Method, assumptions and limits

Fill model

  • single fill price = candle price.close_dollars (last traded YES price)
  • Buy YES: price.close; buy NO: 1 - price.close (binary complement)
  • Spread: yes_ask.close - yes_bid.close, reported as execution-cost estimate and NOT applied to fills
  • candles without a traded price are skipped; no fill is invented
  • entry candle must record volume > 0
  • candlesticks doc · orderbook doc · fixed-point doc

Exit model

  • Settlement: settlement_value_dollars from data/latest/markets_settled.json
  • Mark: recorded traded YES price at the horizon candle (marked, not settled)
  • settlement doc · candles doc

What this cannot tell you

  • Kalshi documents that the demo environment uses mock funds and that demo prices may not reflect real markets. demo env doc
  • Fills are modelled from minute/hour/day candle closes, not from individual trade prints, so entry timing is coarser than a live trader's.
  • All trades are 1 contract; slippage and depth are not modelled.
  • Fees use the official production fee formula; whether the demo environment charges fees is unverified.
  • The resolved sample is 2 markets and 4 trades — far too small to establish an edge.
